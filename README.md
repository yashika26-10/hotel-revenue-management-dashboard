# Hotel Revenue Management Dashboard

An interactive data analytics project for analyzing hotel revenue, room occupancy, pricing performance, and monthly trends using Power BI and Microsoft Excel.

## Project Objectives

- Analyze hotel room occupancy and booking performance.
- Track monthly revenue trends.
- Evaluate Average Daily Rate (ADR) and Revenue per Available Room (RevPAR).
- Compare revenue and occupancy across hotel properties and room classes.
- Identify business insights using interactive visualizations and Excel analysis.

## Tools and Technologies

- **Power BI Desktop** — dashboard development and visualization
- **DAX** — KPI calculations and analytical measures
- **Power Query** — data transformation and preparation
- **Microsoft Excel** — PivotTables, Booking Date Timeline, and business insights

## Dataset Overview

- **Booking records:** 134,590
- **Date coverage:** April–July 2022
- **Key fields:** Booking date, property ID, room category, booking status, and revenue

The analysis is limited to the available four-month period; it does not represent a full-year seasonal comparison.

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Room Occupancy and Hotel Overview
Displays KPI cards for total bookings, realized revenue, occupancy, ADR, and RevPAR, along with monthly trends and booking-status analysis.

### 2. Revenue Optimisation and Property Performance
Compares property-level revenue, occupancy, RevPAR, and revenue across room classes, with interactive slicers.

### 3. Seasonal Trends and Revenue Optimisation
Presents monthly occupancy, revenue, ADR, and RevPAR trends, along with revenue contribution by room class.

## Excel Analysis

The companion Excel workbook includes PivotTables for monthly revenue, property-level revenue, booking platforms, and booking status. A Booking Date Timeline supports time-based analysis, while a Business Insights sheet documents key observations and data limitations.

## Key Findings

- Revenue increased sharply from April to May and declined in July.
- Occupancy decreased between May and July.
- ADR and RevPAR declined in July compared with June.
- Elite and Premium room classes contributed approximately 59.8% of realized revenue in the analyzed data.

## Data Limitations

The dataset covers April–July 2022, so conclusions about seasonality are limited to this period. April ADR and RevPAR are displayed as blank where the required comparable denominator is unavailable.
